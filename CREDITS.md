## Natural ambience

[Park ambiences](https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences) — Thimras, [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Field recordings of birds, open-field wind and a small river cascade. This project uses selected, level-adjusted excerpts with loop crossfades; the cascade is downmixed to mono for positional playback. Original recordings and transformation hashes remain in the editable project.

## Elevation

Terrain Tiles from Mapzen on AWS. United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program. No USGS endorsement is implied.

The mountain relief was cropped, rotated, compressed and blended into an authored meadow basin. The landscape is not a literal reconstruction of the source location. Snow, meadow relief and scene composition are authored.
